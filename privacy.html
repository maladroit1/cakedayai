Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data CakeDayAI ("the app", "we") collects, why we collect it, and how we handle it. It applies to the iOS app published under bundle identifier com.birthdayai.app.

Summary

  • We collect the minimum amount of contact data needed to remind you about birthdays and help you compose messages.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train any AI model.
  • You can delete your data at any time by signing out of the app or contacting us at the address below.

What we collect

When you grant CakeDayAI access to your Contacts and choose to import birthdays, the app reads and stores the following fields for the contacts you import:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth (only when present on the contact card)
  • Primary email address (only when present)
  • Primary phone number (only when present)
  • A stable external identifier we use to deduplicate against your device's address book

We also store account-level information you provide (email address used to sign in) and an Apple push notification token so we can deliver birthday reminders.

The app reads calendar events with your permission to find birthday events you've added to Calendar, but the calendar events themselves are not stored on our servers.

Where your data lives

Contact data is synced to our backend, which runs on Supabase. Supabase stores it encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not share this data with any other third party except as described under "AI message generation" below.

AI message generation

When you ask CakeDayAI to draft a birthday message, we send the recipient's first name, the relationship label you've assigned (e.g. "friend", "sibling"), the tone you've picked, and an optional age to Anthropic's Claude API in order to generate the message text. Anthropic processes this transiently to fulfill the request and does not use it to train their models, per their commercial terms. We do not send phone numbers, email addresses, or other contact fields to Anthropic.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect location, browsing history, search history, or device advertising identifiers.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites. The app's NSUserTrackingUsageDescription is present for completeness but the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown because we do not track.
  • We don't collect health, financial, or government identifier information.

Your rights and choices

  • Revoke permissions. You can revoke Contacts, Calendar, and Notifications permission at any time in iOS Settings â CakeDayAI.
  • Delete your data. Sign out from inside the app to delete the contact records we have synced for you. To delete your account entirely, email the address below and we'll remove everything within 30 days.
  • Export. Email us and we'll send you a copy of the data we hold about you.

Children

CakeDayAI is rated 4+ on the App Store and is appropriate for all ages, but the service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in-app before the change takes effect.
