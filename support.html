Common questions

The app says my contacts have no birthdays — what now?

CakeDayAI reads the "Birthday" field from your iOS Contacts. If a contact doesn't have a birthday saved, it won't appear. Open the Contacts app, edit the contact, and add a birthday — CakeDayAI will pick it up the next time it syncs.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → CakeDayAI → make sure Allow Notifications is on, and that Banners/Sounds are enabled. Inside CakeDayAI, Settings → tap "Test Notification" to confirm delivery.

How do I stop CakeDayAI from accessing my contacts?

iOS Settings → CakeDayAI → toggle Contacts off. Your synced contacts can be deleted from our backend by signing out inside the app, or by emailing us at the address above.

How does the AI message generation work?

When you tap "Generate" on a contact's card, we send the contact's first name, the relationship label you've set, and the tone you've chosen to Anthropic's Claude API. Claude returns a draft you can edit before sending. We never send phone numbers or email addresses. See our Privacy Policy for the full data flow.

I want my account and data deleted.

Email k.melby@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and we'll wipe everything within 30 days.
